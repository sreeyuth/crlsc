Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

The Chalk River Social Club ("the Club") is a volunteer-run organization. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through crlsc.ca.

What we collect

  • Contact details you provide when registering for an event (name and email address).
  • Registration details (number of adult and child tickets, amount paid, payment confirmation status).
  • Whether you chose to receive messages about future Club events.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers on this site, and we do not collect payment card numbers. Payments are made by Interac e-Transfer directly to the Club bank account.

How we use it

  • To manage event registration, headcounts and catering.
  • To contact you about the event you registered for.
  • To send news of future events, only if you opted in.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is accessible only to Club organizers and executives.

Storage and retention

Registration details (name, email, tickets) are stored with access limited to signed-in Club organizers. They are deleted automatically 30 days after the event date.

If you tick the box to hear about future events, we keep your email address only, with the date you agreed and the event you agreed at. Your name is not stored on that list. You can be removed at any time, and every message we send will include a way to unsubscribe.

Your choices

You may ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent to future emails, at any time by contacting us below.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including restricting administrative access. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected people if a breach poses a real risk of significant harm.

Contact

Privacy questions: crsocclb@cnl.ca.
